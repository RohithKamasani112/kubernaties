KQ_PG_01 – Extra Scroll Space in Panels
Context:
Occurs in Playground → Components when interacting with Workloads or Networking side panels.

Problem:
After all list items are loaded, users can still scroll further down into an empty area. This means there’s unnecessary space at the bottom, making it feel like more content should load – but nothing does.

Expected Behavior:

The scroll bar should appear only if the list's height exceeds the visible container.

Once everything is visible, no scrollable empty space should exist.

The scroll should end exactly at the last visible item.

Why It's a Problem:
Unnecessary scrolling confuses users and suggests broken or incomplete loading behavior. It affects UX quality and polish.
